# Collections of agent skills we looked at

A survey from October 2026 of public collections of agent skills. It was a
search for ideas, not a list of dependencies: the template bundles none of
these, and a project picks the few skills its work needs.

The links are as recorded during the survey. They were not pinned to a
version, and a repository may have moved or changed since.

| # | Collection | How it relates to the template |
|---:|---|---|

Two audit skills were looked at separately and are described on the
[research page](https://taspinar.github.io/agentic-coding-template/research.html):
the [Cloudflare security audit skill](https://github.com/cloudflare/security-audit-skill)
and [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable).

A registry that selects and versions skills per project and per role is one of
the [possible improvements](https://taspinar.github.io/agentic-coding-template/future.html);
it is not built.
